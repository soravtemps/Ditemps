📖 Our Story

Ditemps was started by Sorabh Sabal with one simple idea — surprises should feel personal, not generic. What began as a few hand-coded birthday pages for friends and family slowly grew into a full library of interactive, shareable web templates used by people across the country to celebrate the moments that matter most to them.

Every template on this platform is built from scratch, tested on real phones, and designed to load fast even on slow internet connections — because a surprise shouldn't feel like a chore to open.

Sorabh Sabal personally reviews every new template design before it goes live, making sure it meets the same standard of quality, emotion, and polish that Ditemps was founded on.

💬 A Note from Sorabh Sabal

"I built Ditemps because I wanted sending a surprise to feel as special as receiving one. Every template here is made with the same care I'd put into a gift for someone I love. Thank you for trusting us to be a part of your celebrations." — Sorabh Sabal, Founder

🎯 Our Mission

To make heartfelt, interactive surprises accessible to everyone — no design skills or coding knowledge required. We believe the best surprises come from thought and effort, not expensive gifts, and our templates exist to make that effort easy to express.

🔭 Our Vision

To become the most trusted name in digital celebration templates — a place people think of first whenever a birthday, anniversary, proposal, or special occasion is coming up.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know coding to use a template?

No. Just pick a template, fill in the name, message, and photos, and share the generated link.

Does the person receiving the surprise need an account?

No. Only you, as the sender, need to log in to create and save your card. The recipient just opens the link.

What is the difference between Free, Paid, and Premium templates?

Free templates are simple and fully usable at no cost. Paid templates include extra polish and features. Premium templates are our most exclusive, highest-effort designs.

Are my photos and messages safe?

Yes. Your data is stored securely and access is restricted using strict backend security rules — see the section below for details.

Can I get a custom template made for me?

Reach out through the contact details below and Sorabh Sabal's team will get back to you about custom requests.

🔒 Data & Security

We take the privacy of your names, messages, and photos seriously. Login is handled securely through Google Sign-In, and all card and order data is stored in Firestore behind strict, role-based security rules — meaning only you can see your own data, and only the admin account can view orders and the full user list.

  • Every signed-in user can only read and write their own card and order records.
  • Only the verified admin account can read the orders and users collections.
  • No visitor can create, edit, or delete another person's data, even if they try directly through the database.
  • These rules are enforced on Firebase's servers, not just hidden in the app — so they can't be bypassed from the browser.
